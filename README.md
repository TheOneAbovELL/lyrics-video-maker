# Lyric Video Maker

A small, browser-based tool for turning a song and its lyrics into a synced lyric video. Add timed lyrics or tap along as your track plays, then customize the animation and layout to suit the song.

## Get started

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `lyric_video_maker.html` in a modern browser.
3. Choose an audio or video file and add your lyrics.
4. Use timestamped `.lrc` lyrics, or play the track and tap to sync each line.
5. Preview and customize the video, then record it or open clean view to capture your screen.

## Customize

Choose from five lyric animations, several highlight colors, and vertical, square, or widescreen layouts. Adjust text size and position, video darkness, and timing offset. Video tracks appear behind the lyrics; audio tracks use an animated backdrop.

Your media is loaded locally in the browser and is not uploaded by the app. Recording availability and output formats depend on browser support.
